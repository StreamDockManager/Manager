Recovery and disk space

Snapshots cover managed settings and records. Media files, installed packages, and OS credentials require their own backups. Preview and restore a snapshot in Configuration recovery, or use the CLI while Manager is closed.

Recovery cleanup keeps up to 32 files or 1 GiB across all files and nested folders, including older backups and quarantine evidence. Oldest files go first; the newest validated snapshot is always kept. Cleanup waits for active recovery to finish, and may temporarily exceed these limits for safety.
